## 📋 SEO BEST PRACTICES FOLLOWED

### ✅ DO (All Implemented):

- ✅ Natural anchor text variation
- ✅ Contextual link placement
- ✅ Mix of link types (content, navigation, CTA)
- ✅ User-focused, valuable content
- ✅ Mobile responsive design
- ✅ Fast page load (Astro optimized)
- ✅ Schema markup for relationships
- ✅ Target="_blank" for external links
- ✅ Rel="noopener" for security
- ✅ Vietnamese language content
- ✅ Professional UI/UX design
- ✅ Clear CTAs throughout

### ❌ DON'T (All Avoided):

- ❌ No link spam (>10/page)
- ❌ No exact match anchor over-optimization
- ❌ No reciprocal link schemes
- ❌ No links from irrelevant pages
- ❌ No nofollow on all links
- ❌ No hidden or deceptive links
